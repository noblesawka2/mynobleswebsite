“I already have a bank app. Why do I need Nobles Vault?”

Because they serve different purposes. A bank app manages everyday transactions. Nobles Vault connects you to your cooperative membership, contribution plans, goals and eligible services—with human support.

How to Get Started

Follow these steps to download and set up Nobles Vault.

1
Find Nobles Vault

Open the Google Play Store and search for "Nobles Vault".

2
Download the App

Tap Install and wait for the download to finish.

3
Open Nobles Vault

When the download is complete, tap Open. Nobles Vault will launch.

4
Already Have an Account?

If you already have a Nobles Cooperative account, tap Login.

5
New to Nobles?

If you do not have a Nobles Cooperative account, tap Get Started or Sign Up.

6
Complete Your Sign-Up

As a new member, provide all the information requested in the app to reach your dashboard.

7
Need Help?

If you encounter any issues, email support@mynoblescooperative.com.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the Nobles Vault App.

Yes, the Nobles Vault App is completely free to download from the Google Play Store. There are no charges for downloading or installing the app.

You can download and explore the app without being a member. However, to start saving and using the full features, you'll need to complete the registration process through the app.

Yes, the Nobles Vault App uses identity verification, secure account access and transaction controls designed to help protect member information and account activity.

The app provides access to all Nobles Cooperative savings products including EasySave, Future Fund, Gold Vault, EduSave, Group Savings, Monthly Savings Challenge, and Food Vault.
