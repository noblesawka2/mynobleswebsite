Why Trust Nobles?

Your money relationship should begin with trust.

Clear identity, approved channels, responsible assessment and real people you can reach.

Talk to Member CareRead our FAQs

Who we are

Nobles is a registered, member-based cooperative in Anambra State — Reg. No. AN 17915. We are not a commercial bank or instant-loan app.

Where to find us

We provide physical member support in Awka, Anambra State. Confirm current visiting hours before travelling.

How we protect members

We use identity verification, secure account access, transaction controls, privacy practices and approved payment channels designed to protect member information and activity.

How credit works

No loan is automatic. Eligibility, verification, purpose and affordability are assessed. A loan should improve your situation—not make financial pressure worse.

Charges and terms

Applicable registration fees, separate share subscriptions, product rules, charges and repayment terms are explained before you commit or pay.

Official contact

Use this website, the official Nobles Vault listing, +234 915 114 2355 and approved office channels. Verify unexpected messages before acting.

How to complain

Use our complaints route, contact Member Care or visit the Awka office.

What we never request socially

Never send your PIN, OTP, password or full card details through social media, public comments or an unverified chat.

Our promises—and our limits

No guaranteed loan approval. No guaranteed-return language. No hidden unofficial fees. No shame-based collection. Clear information before you commit.

Plan. Save. Build. Grow — with dignity.
Nobles is a women-first financial cooperative helping members build financial stability through disciplined savings, responsible credit, practical education, business support and caring human service.
